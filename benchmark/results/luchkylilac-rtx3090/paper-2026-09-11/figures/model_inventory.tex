\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
\par\smallskip
\emph{params} counts the learned weights; \emph{buffers} is what the registered non-learned tensors that are still part of the trained model add on top, which in these nine models is the batch-norm running mean and variance of ResNet-18 (9600 values) and nothing else. Both sides are counted the same way and agree exactly on both columns for ResNet-18.
\par\smallskip
Excluded from both columns on both sides, as inputs or as tables rebuilt from the config rather than anything training produced: ours \texttt{image}, \texttt{rope.cos}, \texttt{rope.sin}, \texttt{rope.p}; torch \texttt{position\_ids}, \texttt{token\_type\_ids}, \texttt{inv\_freq}, \texttt{original\_inv\_freq}, \texttt{num\_batches\_tracked} (a counter, not data), and a module's causal-mask buffer where it keeps one. The input token ids live in the config, not in the checkpoint index, so they are outside our count by construction.
